TunedHand waitlist privacy notice

Version 1.0 · Effective 10 November 2026 · If this English version and the Chinese translation differ, the English version prevails.

  1. Who we are. 陳翰陞, an individual in Taiwan, collects this information. TunedHand is the working name of a project he is preparing. It is not a registered company, and the name may change. Contact, including for any data protection question: tunedhand.tw@gmail.com.
  2. Why. (a) To learn which AI skills people want and what they would pay, so we can decide whether and how to build TunedHand. (b) To tell you when TunedHand opens. (c) If your answers show you might buy, to email you once before we open to ask whether you are still interested. (d) If you say yes to an interview, to invite you to a video interview of about 40 minutes.
  3. What we collect. Your country or region (and the country name you type if you choose "another country"); the languages you use with AI tools; your email; the AI tool you mainly use; the kind of engineering project or workflow you would like to see built and, in your own words, what you want a skill to do; whether you have trained an agent or skill yourself (written prompts and refined it) and roughly how long it took; whether you paid for prompts, skills, GPTs or templates in the past 12 months; the most you might pay at one time for a skill (a New Taiwan dollar scale in the Chinese form, a US dollar scale in the English form); whether you prefer to buy once or subscribe; whether you are willing to be interviewed; how you heard about us, including the channel code in the link you clicked; and when you ticked the consent box. Please don't put sensitive information (health, financial accounts, other people's details) in the free-text answers. Price answers are not orders: there is no payment and no obligation to buy.
  4. Where it is kept and who sees it. Answers are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets in the founder's own Google account. Only the founder can see them. Google provides these tools under its own terms and privacy policy and may process the data in the United States and other countries where it operates. GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this page and records technical data such as your IP address when you visit. Emails other than the launch notice are sent one by one from tunedhand.tw@gmail.com. If we use an email service for the launch notice, we will name it in an updated version of this notice before sending. We publish only totals (for example "40% had paid before"), never anyone's individual answers. If you live in Switzerland: we are in Taiwan, and your information is also disclosed to the United States (Google) and may be processed in the other countries where Google has data centres (listed at https://datacenters.google/locations/); GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) keeps data in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework. We have no separate data protection contract with any of these providers.
  5. We don't sell or share it. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't use it or let anyone else use it for advertising. This page loads nothing from other sites, sets no cookies and has no advertising or analytics code. Because nothing on it tracks you across sites, we treat browsers that send a "Do Not Track" signal the same as others. The sign-up button opens a Google Form on Google's own site in a new tab, where Google may collect technical data (including through cookies) under Google's privacy policy.
  6. What we email you. Only (a) a launch notice, at most two emails; (b) if your answers show you might buy, one personal email before we open asking whether you are still interested (just reply "no thanks" and we stop); and (c) if you said yes, an interview invitation, at most two emails. No newsletter or other marketing unless you agree separately. Every email tells you how to opt out. If you live in the United States or Canada, the law there requires a postal address in the launch notice; until we have one, we cannot send you the launch notice, and your information is deleted on the schedule in section 7.
  7. How long we keep it. We delete your information within 90 days after the launch notice is sent. If TunedHand has not opened by 31 August 2027, or we decide earlier not to go ahead, we delete everything on that date or within 30 days of the decision, whichever comes first. Nothing is kept after 30 November 2027 in any case. Totals that cannot identify anyone are kept.
  8. Your rights. Wherever you live, you can ask to see or get a copy of your information, to correct or complete it, to stop us using it, or to delete it. Email tunedhand.tw@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We reply within 15 days for access or copies and within 30 days for other requests (Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act, art. 13); opt-outs and deletions are done within 3 working days. We won't treat you differently for asking.
  9. How to leave. Use the opt-out in any email, reply "unsubscribe", or email tunedhand.tw@gmail.com. To stay on the waitlist but not be interviewed, reply "no interview".
  10. If you don't answer. Without your email and the required answers we can't add you to the waitlist. There is no other consequence.
  11. Who this is for. You must be 18 or older. We do not serve the EU, the EEA (including Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein), the UK or mainland China; please don't sign up if you live there. We rely on the country you choose and don't verify it, so this is a reasonable effort, not a guarantee. If you do sign up from there, or if your country is unclear, we delete your answers within 7 days and won't contact you.
  12. Changes. We change this notice only by publishing a new version with a new effective date. A new project name or a new contact address is published this way and does not change how we use your information. If we want to use your information for anything new, or keep it longer than section 7 says, we will email you first and ask for your consent again.
